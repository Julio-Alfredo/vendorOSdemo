Before you start

A few things to know.

Does it work on my iPhone?

The app is designed for mobile. Open the pilot in Safari while online, use Share → Add to Home Screen, then open it once online and wait for “Offline ready.” Test it in airplane mode before relying on it at an event. Downloaded HTML files are browser-dependent on iPhone; use the installed website there.

Is the free pilot different from the paid product?

The current public pilot includes the features shown here. A separate paid release and its delivery terms are not available yet. You can try the pilot now without buying anything.

Will my data stay saved?

Records save on the device and browser you use. Clearing browser data or changing browsers can remove or separate your records. Export backups regularly; the app does not automatically sync across devices.

Will it tell me exactly how much I’ll make?

No. Forecasts depend on your assumptions and history. Scenario ranges illustrate different transaction counts; they do not guarantee earnings. Review actual costs after each event.

Does it process customer payments or replace bookkeeping?

No. The sales log records amounts you enter. It does not charge cards, file taxes or automatically deduct stock. Product prep currently assumes one item per transaction.

How do I share feedback?

Email Chris Brown with what was useful, what was confusing and what you would need before purchasing. You do not need to share private financial figures.
